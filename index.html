About Me

I am a third-year student studying Finance and Data Science at Northeastern University, I will be graduating in December 2027. I’m from South Korea, and I grew up speaking Korean and English, which has influenced the way I think about people, culture, and business. Moving between two languages, I learned early on that the same idea can look very different depending on who is explaining it and who is listening.

My educational interests lie at the crossroads of numbers and decisions. I am particularly interested in corporate finance: why does a company decide to raise money in a certain way; how does a company decide which projects are worth investing in; how do leaders balance risk and growth? Data science gives me the tools to look at those questions with evidence instead of guesswork, whether that means cleaning a messy dataset, building a model, or designing a clear visualization that helps someone understand a result at a glance.

Outside of class, I enjoy working out and being active. Regular training keeps me disciplined and provides a reset from long days of coursework. This site is a small project for my visualizing data course, and a place to share a little more about where I come from. Please use the menu above to visit my page on Seoul, South Korea's capital city. It contains photos, a list of places worth visiting and an interactive chart of the city's seasons.
