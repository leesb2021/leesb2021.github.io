About Me

I am a third-year student at Northeastern University in Boston, studying Finance and Data Science, and I expect to graduate in December 2027. I originally come from South Korea, and I grew up speaking both Korean and English, which has shaped how I think about people, culture and business. Moving between two languages taught me early on that the same idea can look very different depending on who is explaining it and who is listening.

My academic interests sit at the point where numbers meet decisions. I am especially curious about how corporations make financial decisions: why a company chooses to raise money one way instead of another, how it decides which projects deserve investment, and how leaders weigh risk against growth. Data science gives me the tools to look at those questions with evidence instead of guesswork, whether that means cleaning a messy dataset, building a model or designing a clear visualization that helps someone understand a result at a glance.

Outside of class, I enjoy working out and staying active. Training regularly keeps me disciplined and gives me a reset from long days of coursework. This website is a small project for my data visualization course, and it is also a place to share a little more about where I come from. Use the menu above to visit my page about Seoul, the capital of South Korea, where you can find photos, a list of places worth visiting and an interactive chart of the city's seasons.
